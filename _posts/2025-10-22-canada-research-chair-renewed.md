---
title: Jon's Canada Research Chair is renewed
ref: news-crc-2025
summary: >-
  Jonathan Britt's Tier 2 Canada Research Chair in Behavioural Neuroscience has
  been renewed.
---
In October 2025, McGill announced that Jon's Canada Research Chair (Tier 2) in Behavioural
Neuroscience, funded through the Canadian Institutes of Health Research, has
been renewed. The chair supports the lab's work on the neural circuits that
control motivated behaviour.
