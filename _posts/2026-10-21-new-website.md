The Britt Lab has a new website. Alongside an updated research overview and
our current team, it includes a [gallery](/gallery/) where you can explore
published datasets directly: serotonin's effect on attention, thousands of
behavioural trials from a single mouse, and genome-wide methylation changes.

The site is bilingual. Use the language switch at the top of any page to read
it in French.
