---
title: Serotonin and the strength of attention
ref: news-serotonin-attention
summary: >-
  A collaboration led by the Krishnaswamy lab, published in Nature
  Communications, shows that fast serotonin dynamics regulate how strongly
  mice attend to visual targets.
doi: "10.1038/s41467-026-70658-y"
---
Using optical recordings and optogenetic manipulations in mice performing a
cued visual detection task, Lehnert and colleagues found that rapid dynamics of
dorsal raphe serotonin neurons and cortical serotonin release selectively
regulate the *strength* of attention. Brief suppression of these neurons
increased attentional strength and performance; brief excitation reduced both.

The paper's source data and an example dataset are public. You can explore
both in our [gallery](/gallery/#data).
