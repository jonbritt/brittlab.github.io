---
title: Does alcohol taste better the more you drink it?
ref: news-alcohol-palatability
summary: >-
  In Physiology & Behavior, Valyear, Eustachon and Britt report that mice come
  to find alcohol, and flavours paired with it, more palatable over time.
doi: "10.1016/j.physbeh.2024.114500"
---
The taste and smell of alcoholic drinks are potent relapse triggers, partly
because they come to seem more palatable with repeated drinking. By analysing
the microstructure of licking, Milan Valyear, Noémie Eustachon and Jon found
that lick bouts for alcohol gradually lengthen relative to water, and that an
alcohol-paired flavour partly maintains this in extinction: a sign that the
flavour itself has gained palatability.
